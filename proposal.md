- Question: Can UV-Vis absorption spectra be used to predict the concentrations of multiple chemical compounds in a mixture?
- Data: Chemcial_Data_for_PLS.csv , https://zenodo.org/records/19324549?\n
- Is the data sufficient?

The CSV contains 846 observations with each row representing a sample with corresponding chemical concentration measurements and an absorbance spectrum.

There are three variable types: chemical concentrations, UV-Vis spectra, and sample information. The variables are bromide, fluopyram, diflufenican, and mesosulfuron. There are also absorbance measurements from 200-700 nm, and sample information including volume, port, season, soil, and storm.

Missing information:
Units of information, measurement methods, detection limits, spectral values
